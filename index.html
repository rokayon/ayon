I am an AI/ML researcher who fine-tunes and adapts deep learning models, working mainly with lightweight CNNs, hybrid CNN–Transformer architectures, and fine-tuned NLP models. I have co-authored 15+ peer-reviewed papers, including five Q1 journal papers (one published, two accepted, two under review), created nine open research datasets with two more in preparation, and reviewed 15 manuscripts for Springer Nature, Elsevier, and Frontiers journals. I am seeking higher studies opportunities in AI-related fields.

My work pairs efficient models with explainable AI methods such as Grad-CAM, SHAP, and pseudo-segmentation, so each prediction can be checked rather than taken on trust. I apply this across healthcare, agriculture, and Bengali language and speech.
